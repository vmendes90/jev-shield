# Brag Plan: Jev Shield

## What is this app?
Jev Shield is an open-source, privacy-first Chrome Extension (Manifest V3) that semantically detects and blocks native feed ads, sponsored posts, and stealth promotions using TypeSafe's Jev AI model, backed by a dual-layer media protection engine.

## The angle
Filterlists like uBlock Origin are powerless against native ads on modern social networks because sponsored posts share the exact same first-party domain, markup structure, and styling as organic content. Jev Shield brings calibrated System One AI directly into the browser to read the text, judge the intent in sub-100ms, and collapse stealth promotions before you even read them.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Text cuts in over dark sleek aesthetic: *"Traditional ad blockers read URLs. What happens when the ad is the post itself?"*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Scan & Collapse**: A stealth sponsored feed post is scanned by Jev, flagged with 94% ad probability, and instantly collapses into the branded reveal badge (`🛡️ Native Ad hidden by Jev [94% ad]`).
- **The Dashboard HUD**: The popup dashboard appears with live counters counting up: `15 Blocked`, `Cache Efficiency: 25%`, `Jev API Calls: 3`.
- **The Power Toggles**: The `Deep AI Scan` toggle turns on with an animated toggle flip, demonstrating zero telemetry and 100% client-side BYOK security.

## Outro / punchline
The glowing 🛡️ Jev Shield logo settles with clean typography: *"Open source. Zero telemetry. Stop reading ads dressed like content."* CTA pointing to `github.com/vmendes90/jev-shield`.

## User flow worth showing
Feed scrolling with native post → Jev evaluates candidate text in ~40ms → Instant collapse into "🛡️ Native Ad hidden by Jev" badge → Popup HUD tracks live blocked metrics.
